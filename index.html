What We Offer

Comprehensive Services for the Food & Beverage Industry

We cover every stage of your compliance and business journey — from initial registration through international expansion, quality improvement, and beyond.

FSSAI Registration & Licensing

Complete FSSAI registration, state licensing, and central licensing for food businesses of all categories. Renewals and modifications handled seamlessly.

GST Registration & Filing

GST registration, regular filing, and ongoing compliance management for food manufacturers, traders, and exporters.

Import Export Code (IEC)

IEC registration with DGFT for businesses looking to import or export food and beverage products.

Udyam (MSME) Registration

Udyam registration for micro, small, and medium enterprises to access government schemes, subsidies, and credit benefits.

Trademark Registration

Brand protection through trademark filing, monitoring, and renewal for food brands, product names, and logos.

Gumasta / Shop Act License

Local business registration and shop establishment licensing for food business operations in Maharashtra and Gujarat.

RCMC Certificate

Registration-Cum-Membership Certificate from Export Promotion Councils (EPC) required for availing export benefits and schemes.

Legal Metrology Registration

Compliance with Legal Metrology Act for packaged commodities — weight, measure, and labelling declarations.

Icegate & AD Code Registration

Customs portal registration and AD Code registration with banks for smooth export-import transactions and IGST refunds.

EPR Registration

Extended Producer Responsibility registration for plastic packaging waste compliance with CPCB guidelines.

AGMARK & BIS Registration

Quality certification under AGMARK for agricultural commodities and BIS registration for standardised food products.

Factory & Environmental NOC

Factory license, fire NOC, CPCB/SPCB environmental compliance registration for food processing facilities.

ISO 22000:2018 – Food Safety

Implementation and certification for Food Safety Management Systems covering the entire food supply chain.

ISO 9001:2015 – Quality Management

Quality Management System certification for consistent product quality, customer satisfaction, and operational excellence.

HACCP Certification

Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Points — systematic approach to identifying and controlling food safety hazards.

GMP Certification

Good Manufacturing Practices certification ensuring product quality, hygiene, and consistent production standards.

Halal Certification

Halal certification for food manufacturers targeting Muslim-majority markets in India and internationally.

Organic Certification

India Organic, NPOP, NOP (US), and EU Organic certifications for organic food producers and processors.

FSSC 22000 / BRC / IFS / SQF

International retailer and food safety scheme certifications required for global supply chain entry and supermarket listings.

Kosher Certification

Kosher certification for food products targeting Jewish consumer markets in the US, Europe, and Israel.

ISO 14001 / 45001 / 50001

Environmental, Occupational Health & Safety, and Energy Management System certifications for responsible operations.

US FDA Registration

FDA facility registration, Prior Notice submission, and US food import compliance for Indian food exporters.

EU, China & Russia Certificates

Country-specific food import certificates and health certificates for European, Chinese, and Russian markets.

APEDA Registration

APEDA registration for exporters of agricultural and processed food products to avail export promotion support.

All Types of Laboratory Testing

Coordination with NABL-accredited labs for microbiological, chemical, physical, and nutritional testing of food products.

Nutritional Analysis & Labelling

Nutritional value computation per 100g/100ml and per serve, formatted to FSSAI, EU, US FDA, and international requirements.

Shelf-Life Testing & Studies

Accelerated and real-time shelf-life studies to determine Best Before dates and optimise packaging.

Sensory Evaluation

Trained panel and consumer sensory testing for taste, texture, aroma, and appearance — critical for product launches.

FSSAI Compliance Audits

Internal, mock, and third-party compliance audits against FSSAI Food Safety and Standards regulations.

Warehouse & Cold Storage Inspection

Hygiene, temperature management, pest control, and documentation inspection for warehouses and cold storage facilities.

Food Fraud & Adulteration Assessment

Risk-based food fraud vulnerability assessments and mitigation plans for manufacturers and retailers.

Water Quality Testing

Potable water testing for food processing facilities per BIS and FSSAI standards — mandatory for licensing.

New Product Development

Recipe Formulation & Standardisation

Nutraceutical & Functional Foods

Food Labelling & Packaging Design

Plant Setup & Commissioning
